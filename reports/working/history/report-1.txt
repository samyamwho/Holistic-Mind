Title: Holistic Mind App: A Trauma-Informed Mental Wellness Mobile Application
Abstract /Summary of proposal:
Holistic Mind App is a trauma-informed mobile wellness application designed to
support adults managing childhood trauma, anxiety, and ADHD between therapy
sessions. The app delivers evidence-based tools including guided breathwork, somatic
exercises, and psychoeducation audio content through a calm, non-clinical interface. It
leverages AI-driven personalization to recommend tools based on the user's self-
reported nervous system state, ensuring contextually relevant support at any moment.
Holistic Mind App operates on a freemium model, offering core features for free and
full content access through a premium subscription.

Problem Statements:
1. 2. 3. Currently over 10,000 mental health apps exist on major platforms, yet fewer
than 3% are built around evidence-based trauma frameworks, leaving adults
with complex trauma and ADHD without adequate digital support between
therapy sessions (Linardon et al., 2020).
Currently, fewer than half of adults with mental health conditions received
treatment in 2024 (Grandview, 2025) which leaves millions without support
between or outside of therapy sessions. However, the ideal situation is always-
available, self-directed digital wellness tools. Therefore, there is a gap
regarding accessible mental health apps for adults who cannot access consistent
professional care.
Over 63.9% of adults report at least one Adverse Childhood Experience (CDC,
2023), yet no mainstream wellness app specifically addresses nervous system
dysregulation as a core design principle, which causes trauma survivors to
remain without targeted digital coping tools. However, the ideal situation is a
personalized app that adapts to a user's emotional state in real time.
Aim:
This research aims to address the lack of trauma-informed digital mental health tools
by designing and developing an AI-powered cross-platform mobile application in order
to enable adults affected by childhood trauma and ADHD to self-regulate their nervous
system between therapy sessions.
Objectives: SMART – Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Realistic, Time Bound
1. To design and develop a cross-platform mobile application with a nervous
system check-in system offering 5 emotional states that trigger AI-personalized
tool recommendations.
2. To implement 3 core content modules. Breathwork (10 exercises), Somatic
Tools (8 exercises), and a Psychoeducation Audio Library (2 series, 5 episodes
each).
3. To integrate an AI recommendation engine that maps user emotional state inputs
to relevant tools using a rule-based and preference-learning model.
4. To build a secure freemium access system with Firebase authentication and
differentiated free and premium content tiers.
